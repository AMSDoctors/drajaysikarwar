Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about heart health and our services.

When should I see a cardiologist?

You should consult a cardiologist if you experience symptoms like chest pain, shortness of breath, dizzy spells, or irregular heartbeats. It is also advised if you have risk factors like high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol, or a family history of heart disease.

What symptoms should never be ignored?

Never ignore sudden or severe chest pain, pain radiating to the arm or jaw, extreme shortness of breath, unexplained fainting, or sudden rapid heart palpitations. These could be signs of a medical emergency requiring immediate attention.

Do you treat high blood pressure?

Yes, comprehensive management of hypertension (high blood pressure) is provided, including medication optimization, lifestyle counseling, and monitoring to prevent long-term cardiac damage.

What is angioplasty?

Angioplasty is a minimally invasive procedure used to open clogged heart arteries. A small balloon is temporarily inflated where your artery is clogged to help widen it, often combined with the permanent placement of a small wire mesh tube called a stent to help prop the artery open.

Is ECG available?

Yes, we provide complete diagnostic services including ECG, Echocardiography, TMT, and Holter Monitoring using state-of-the-art equipment.
